Elapsed time, computation and outcomes across the pilot and expanded benchmark. (a) P1b elapsed time for three four-task batches per arm at concurrency two; symbols show the three batches and horizontal lines their means. Mean batch times were 4.451 h (Guided), 3.958 h (Backbone), 3.894 h (Record) and 4.827 h (Brief). (b) Cumulative QM job-hours for the 15 P2 tasks available in all four effort snapshots (15 runs per arm); each point is a task-run and each line is the arm mean. Job-hours sum individual QM process durations; the Backbone M1 value was reconstructed from native CPU time divided by four. (c) Mean completed, failed and cancelled QM jobs per run for the same cohort. (d) Task-paired arrows from Backbone (open circles) to Guided (filled circles) connect recorded computation to final total scores. Open diamonds identify three Backbone graphs reconstructed from their own archived evidence; their released repaired totals are shown. Panels b--d describe computational investment, while panel a measures batch elapsed time.
