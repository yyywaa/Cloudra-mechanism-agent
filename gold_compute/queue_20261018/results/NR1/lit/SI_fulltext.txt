Supporting Information Available  
The Supporting Information for this document includes tables with structures, rotational 
constants, vibrational frequencies and total energies for all optimized geometries. In 
addition, the entropy, zero point correction and thermal correction for formamide, 
reactive intermediates and transition states are available. Additional plots illustrating the 
pressure dependence of RRKM rate constants, 
, are also provided. This information is 
available free of charge via the Internet at http://pubs.acs.org.
